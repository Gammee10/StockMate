# Stage 1: Idea Inbox

**Format Rule:** `"[Who] struggles with [problem] when [situation]."`

---

### Candidate Ideas

1. **The Fast Stockout (Core Idea):**

   > _"Small retail shop owners struggle to know what items are running out in real time when dealing with customer rushes and unable to record sales manually."_

2. **The Payment-to-Stock Blind Spot:**

   > _"Neighborhood shopkeepers struggle to reconcile daily digital payments (Telebirr/Bank SMS) with actual physical stock sold when closing shop at the end of the day."_

3. **The Restock Guesswork:**
   > _"Independent merchants struggle to decide which fast-moving goods to repurchase when visiting wholesale suppliers with limited operating cash."_

---

### Chosen Idea

> **"Small shop owners struggle to keep accurate inventory and track running-out stock when processing fast-paced cash and digital payments during busy hours."**

- **Speed constraint locked:** Entry must take <3 seconds (large tap targets, smart sorting of frequent items, automatic SMS amount matching).
