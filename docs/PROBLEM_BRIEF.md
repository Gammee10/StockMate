# Stage 2: One-Page Problem Brief

## 1. Problem Statement

- **Who:** Independent retail shopkeepers / kiosk operators (selling 100–300 fast-moving items single-handedly).
- **Pain:** They don't know what items are running low until a customer asks for something that isn't on the shelf.
- **When:** During peak rush hours (morning rush and evening post-work rush).
- **How often:** Multiple times daily, every business day.
- **Cost today:**
  - Lost revenue (customers leave empty-handed to a rival shop).
  - Unexplained cash and inventory leaks (money missing at end of day).
  - Wasted time manually recounting shelves after closing.

---

## 2. How They Cope Today (The Real Competitors)

1. **Paper Notebook:** Often abandoned during customer lines; prone to illegible notes and math errors.
2. **Payment SMS List (Telebirr / CBE):** Used strictly as proof of payment, completely disconnected from product quantities.
3. **Counting by Eye:** Reactive inspection when shelves look bare or during idle moments.
4. **Memory:** Remembering what ran out; leads to forgotten restocks at wholesale markets.

---

## 3. Name One Specific User

- **Profile:** _Dawit_, 32.
- **Business:** Runs a busy neighborhood mini-store (kiosk) in Addis Ababa with ~200 items (beverages, packaged foods, household goods).
- **Operation:** Works the counter alone. During rushes, 2 to 4 customers queue simultaneously, paying with a mix of cash and Telebirr QR/transfer.
  _(Note: Can be substituted with an actual contact you can interview)._

---

## 4. Testable Assumptions (Riskiest First)

1. **The 3-Second Friction Barrier (Highest Risk):**
   _A rushed shopkeeper will actually tap an app to record an item rather than just handing over the item and taking money._
2. **Inventory Setup Hurdle:**
   _A shopkeeper will tolerate adding their top 30–50 items into the app initially._
3. **SMS Notification Latency:**
   _Payment SMS messages arrive quickly enough (<5 seconds) to be matched without creating a customer bottleneck._
4. **Trust in Warnings:**
   _A shopkeeper will trust the app's "low stock" alert enough to restock without doing a manual recount._

---

## 5. Success Metric (One Number)

> **Primary Outcome Metric:**
> **Zero unexpected stockouts on top 20 fast-moving items over a 2-week evaluation period, backed by $\ge 80\%$ of daily transactions logged in the app.**
