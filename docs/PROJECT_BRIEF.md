# StockMate - Initial Project Brief (Draft)

> **Status:** Initial Proposal (Pre-Stage 1)

## Context & Core Problem
- **Target User:** Shop owners (retail / small shop operators).
- **Core Problem:** Owners do not know what is selling or what is running out in real time. They only discover shortages when a customer asks for an item that is already out of stock.
- **Timing:** Every day, particularly during rush hours when manual recording is impossible.
- **Current Coping Mechanisms:**
  - Paper notebooks (frequently skipped during peak hours).
  - Telebirr / Bank SMS notifications as an informal payment audit trail.
  - Periodic visual inspection ("counting stock by eye").
  - Memory.
- **Business Cost:**
  - Lost revenue from unstocked inventory.
  - Manual errors and unreadable entries in paper books.
  - Unexplained cash leaks / stock shrinkage.

*Note: Functional requirements, constraints, and architecture will be refined through the 10-stage process.*
